# AI Policy

## Summary

You can use AI to write a contribution, or to help you write it. The rules of
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) apply to that contribution, as to any other.

One rule applies only to AI.

## Your own voice

Take part yourself when you talk with the maintainer. An agent can do the coding work
for you. But do not let an agent read and answer the comments on your pull request or
issue while you only pass work to it. Then the maintainer puts more work into the
conversation than you do. The rule is about who takes responsibility for the
conversation, not about who writes the code.

If you do not want to take part yourself, open an issue, not a pull request. Put in the
issue all the information that you gave to your AI. The maintainer can then give that
information to an AI directly, and you do not need to stay in the conversation.

This policy adapts the [AI policy of the Ash project](https://github.com/ash-project/.github/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md).
